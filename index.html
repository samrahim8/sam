About

Hi, I'm Sam Rahim.

I spent the last decade working at high growth startups like Street FC, Switchyards and Guild. Now I run my own shop, Provisions.

I'm from Southern California, and lived there for my first 25 years. When I was in college at UC Irvine, I spent a summer in the jungles of Belize doing archaeology - our team found dozens of artifacts and structures that were thousands of years old.

I began my career at Disney's corporate campus in LA - where, ultimately, I met my wife. We live in Orlando and are expecting our first child imminently!
